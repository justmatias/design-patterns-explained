# Structural Patterns

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/category-structural-green)

Structural patterns deal with **how objects and classes are composed** into larger structures. The goal is to assemble parts into a working whole while keeping those parts loosely coupled, so the structure can change without rippling edits through the rest of the code.

They answer questions like: how do I make two incompatible interfaces work together, how do I add behavior to an object without subclassing it, and how do I represent a part-whole hierarchy so clients can treat individual objects and compositions uniformly? Instead of inheritance alone, these patterns lean on composition to keep designs flexible.
