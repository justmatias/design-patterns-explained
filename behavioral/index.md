# Behavioral Patterns

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/category-behavioral-orange)

Behavioral patterns deal with **how objects communicate and distribute responsibility**. They focus on the flow of control and the assignment of behavior between objects, rather than on what objects are or how they are built.

They answer questions like: how do I let an object change its behavior when its state changes, how do I notify many objects about an event without tightly coupling them, and how do I encapsulate a request so it can be queued, logged, or undone? The emphasis is on clear, flexible interactions that keep collaborating objects independent of each other.
